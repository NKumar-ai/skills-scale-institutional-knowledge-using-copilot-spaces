# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers translate user needs and business requirements into intuitive, accessible interfaces and experiences. They champion the user perspective throughout the project lifecycle and collaborate closely with Product and Development teams.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and visual interfaces
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and accessibility standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature scoping and user needs validation
- Work with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Document design decisions and maintain design systems/guidelines

### Goals
- Create user-centric, accessible experiences
- Reduce design-to-implementation friction
- Continuously improve UX based on user feedback and metrics

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Product and Developers
- User research findings and insights
- Design documentation and handoffs to development
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Managers:** Partner on defining user acceptance criteria, validating product hypotheses through user research, and prioritizing features based on user impact
- **With Developers:** Collaborate during implementation to ensure design fidelity, provide design specifications and assets, and participate in code reviews for UI components
- **With QA:** Define acceptance criteria related to user experience and validate that implemented features match design intent

---

## Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile practices and remove impediments that block the team's progress. They act as servant leaders, coaching the team on continuous improvement and process adherence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on Agile practices and principles
- Track and report on sprint velocity and team health metrics
- Foster psychological safety and a blameless, learning-focused culture
- Monitor process adherence and suggest improvements

### Goals
- Optimize team velocity and delivery cadence
- Minimize context switching and interruptions
- Build a high-performing, collaborative team culture

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of Agile ceremonies
- Blocker escalation and resolution tracking
- Team health and velocity reports
- Retrospective action item follow-up

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Managers:** Collaborate on timeline management, risk escalation, and ensuring team capacity aligns with project commitments
- **With Developers:** Support through daily sync facilitation, blocker removal, and coaching on technical practices
- **With Product Managers:** Ensure sprint scope is realistic and negotiate priority changes that impact delivery timelines

---

## Technical Writers

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain clear, accurate documentation that helps teams and users understand systems, processes, and features. They ensure consistency and accessibility of written communications across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Author and maintain user guides, system documentation, and API documentation
- Create and update release notes, changelog entries, and deployment guides
- Contribute to clarity and consistency in all written project artifacts
- Collaborate with teams to capture and document decisions and processes
- Maintain documentation standards and style guides
- Ensure documentation is accessible and updated throughout the project

### Goals
- Reduce onboarding time and support burden through clear documentation
- Improve communication clarity across technical and non-technical audiences
- Maintain a single source of truth for process and system documentation

### Typical Communication
- Documentation drafts and review cycles
- Process documentation and decision records
- Release note contributions and maintenance
- Participation in project kickoff and retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers:** Partner on technical accuracy, gather implementation details, and ensure code documentation aligns with system architecture
- **With Project Managers:** Document project processes, decisions, and lessons learned in retrospectives
- **With Product Managers:** Create user-facing documentation and feature guides aligned with product messaging

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They elicit, document, and validate requirements, ensuring clear communication between business stakeholders and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Define use cases, user stories, and business process flows
- Perform gap analysis between business needs and current solutions
- Create and maintain requirements documentation and traceability matrices
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business objectives
- Identify risks and dependencies related to business requirements

### Goals
- Ensure requirements clarity and completeness before development
- Reduce rework and scope creep through thorough analysis
- Maximize business value delivered by the team

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and specifications
- Stakeholder interviews and discovery sessions
- Use case and user story documentation
- Change request and impact analysis reports

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Managers:** Collaborate on translating business strategy into prioritized features and acceptance criteria
- **With Developers:** Clarify requirements, answer implementation questions, and validate that technical solutions meet business intent
- **With Stakeholders:** Facilitate communication, gather feedback, and communicate progress toward business objectives

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Cross-functional collaboration is essential—understand how each role depends on and supports others for successful project delivery.
